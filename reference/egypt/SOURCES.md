# Egyptian reference screenshots: sources

All files are real in-game captures of **Age of Mythology: Retold** (2024–2026). There is no fan art, no AoE2/AoE3/AoE4 and no original-2002 AoM graphics. I opened and checked each one.
Sources: the official pantheon page (ageofempires.com, WebP converted to JPEG); the Age of Empires Series Wiki's own in-game crops (Retold uploads, PNG converted to JPEG); untouched images from official-forum bug threads and one Steam community screenshot.
Images wider than 2000 px were downscaled. No other edits were made.

## Gaps

- I found no capture of the god-pick screen with **Ra, Isis or Set selected** (their focus text and minor-god tree). ui_04 shows the Egyptian row with Gaia selected, and ui_05 shows a Sobek row crop. The text of each god's bonuses is in EGYPT.md section 4.
- I found no capture of the god powers **Ancestors**, **Citadel** or **Meteor** while they are being cast. power_10 shows the Son of Osiris result, myth_10 the Minions that Ancestors summons, and power_13 the Meteor craters.
- YouTube frames could not be fetched (the video host refused the download from this machine). Thumbnails were not used.
- Pharaoh/Priest close-ups also exist as 120 px wiki icons, which were not used. unit_04, unit_09 and unit_10 cover them.
