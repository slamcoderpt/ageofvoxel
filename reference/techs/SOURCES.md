# Armory / Market / Temple reference screenshots: sources

All files are real in-game captures of Age of Mythology: Retold (2024–2026). No fan art, no AoE2 or AoE4, and no original-2002 AoM. Each one was opened and checked.
The fandom images are the wiki's own in-game crops (Retold, uploaded October 2024), converted from WebP to JPEG. The forum images are untouched originals from bug reports and discussion threads on the official forum, converted to JPEG.

## Gaps

- I found no full-screen capture of a **Greek** Armory selected with its tech row. ui_02 is the Egyptian Armory: same panel and generic row, but different god techs.
  The Greek god techs would be Phobos, Deimos, Enyo, Sarissa, Aegis, Sun Ray, Shafts of Plague, Forge of Olympus, Olympian Weapons and Harvest of Souls.
- I found no capture of the Temple with a full tech row. ui_04 shows the Greek Temple panel at low resolution.
